Yangyi Huang 黄洋逸

I am a Ph.D. student at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, working with Weiyang Liu at Sphere Lab.

My research focuses on training and adapting foundation models, especially post-training, parameter-efficient adaptation, and model geometry. I study how models acquire new capabilities while retaining existing ones.

Previously, I worked on 3D vision and generative modeling, including research at NVIDIA. I received my master’s and bachelor’s degrees from Zhejiang University, where I worked with Deng Cai.
